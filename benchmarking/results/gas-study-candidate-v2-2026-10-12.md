Generated by `benchmarking/gas-study.cjs` on 2026-10-12T19:46:49.022Z. In-process Hardhat network, no other traffic. Contracts of candidate v2 (the working tree). **Nothing here was measured on Sepolia or on any real network.**

## 2. Insertion gas by leaf index, depth 25

The same call with the same calldata at chosen leaf indices. Before each call the tree's counter was set to the index and every `filledSubtrees` slot was given a value or emptied as in a tree that grew to that size (level k is first written at leaf 2^k − 1). The rule:

> gas(i) = base + 2,079 × (number of 1 bits of i − 1) + 17,100 if i = 2^k − 1

| Call | Base (one 1 bit) | Indices measured | Largest difference from the rule | Largest gas measured | at leaf index |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `NoteTreeDepthHarness.insert`, depth 25 | 823,060 | 84 | 0 | 887,977 | 16,777,215 (2^24 − 1) |
| `GlobalReputation.depositPayment` | 853,303 | 84 | 0 | 918,220 | 16,777,215 (2^24 − 1) |

The rule is exact for every index below 2^25 − 1: the largest difference is 0 gas over 84 indices on the harness and 84 on `depositPayment` (all 25 indices 2^k − 1, their neighbours, one index for each number of 1 bits from 2 to 24, and the fill levels 1,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000). The last leaf of the tree, index 2^25 − 1, has no left-child level and writes no `filledSubtrees` slot: it costs 5,049 gas less than the rule says (898,150 for `depositPayment`).

- **2,079 gas per 1 bit.** At a level where the path is a right child the loop reads `noteFilledSubtrees[level]`, a slot not yet accessed in the transaction: 2,100 gas (EIP-2929; one such read per 1 bit in the table above). At a left-child level it takes the zero hash from the table in memory and tests the `stored` flag instead. The measured difference between the two branches is 2,079, so the left-child branch executes 21 gas of plain instructions more than the right-child one (this remainder is computed; the instructions were not listed). Every leaf after the dummy note has one 1 bit at least, hence "− 1" in the rule.
- **17,100 gas at an index 2^k − 1.** Each insertion writes one slot, `noteFilledSubtrees[k]` for its lowest left-child level k. That slot is empty until leaf 2^k − 1, whose write therefore costs 22,100 gas (empty to non-empty, first access) instead of 5,000 (non-empty to another value): 17,100 more, once per level in the life of a deployment (25 times in all, at leaves 1, 3, 7, … , 2^24 − 1; the dummy note of the constructor wrote level 0).
- **The base** holds everything that does not depend on the index: 25 calls of the Poseidon contract, the new root written to `noteRoot` (5,000) and recorded in `knownNoteRoots` (22,100, a slot never written before), `noteNextIndex` read and written, the `NoteAdded` event, and the one `noteFilledSubtrees` write (5,000).
- The hasher is called by `STATICCALL` with two words of calldata, and the Poseidon contract executes the same instructions for any input, so the values of the leaf and of the nodes do not change the gas. What does change it, by a few tens of gas between two transactions at the same index, is the calldata of the transaction (zero bytes of a commitment, of a proof or of a signature).

The untimed first proof of each circuit is in the table; it is not a cold start, because the setup that precedes it in this script has already started the curve and the prover's threads. A browser downloads the two files instead; that was not measured.
