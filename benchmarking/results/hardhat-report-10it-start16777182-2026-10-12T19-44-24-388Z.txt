Latency of an on-chain operation runs from the start of its client-side preparation (encryption,
commitments, gas estimation) to the mined receipt. zkProof_mergeWithdraw includes reading the
NoteAdded logs, rebuilding the tree and fullProve. sealDetails is the customer's part of the late
release (pickup key, sealing, encryption of the seal value for the courier); openDetails is the
courier's (reading the Information log, decryption, opening with the pickup key).

NOTES
- Each privacy cycle inserts 3 notes into the note tree (pool deposit, change note, revenue note).
- Every past root stays valid (mapping, O(1)). Each insertion records its new root in a mapping slot
  that was never written (zero -> non-zero SSTORE) and writes one filled-subtree slot.
- Iteration 1 is the first cycle of a new worker. It also pays first writes that later cycles do
  not: Chain.lastOrder, the courier's record (deliveries, ratings), and tree levels whose
  filled-subtree slot is still empty.
- The customer's two strings for the courier are emitted by Storage (event Information) and not stored.
- One order per cycle uses a fresh stealth account; the stipend transfer always creates it.
