  Closed-loop bound: a client needs 2 blocks per order (announce, then makeOrder), so k clients offer at most k/(2 x 12 s) orders/s.

Notes
  - Fresh deployment per run; client wallets are reused across levels, so later levels see a larger note tree and couriers with a record.
  - Contracts: the final ones of 2026-10-12 (ratings in the clear, orders as minimal proxies, the worker's ciphertexts in an event, the establishment paid by a note in the pool). Payloads: the dApps' late-release format.
  - Warm-up: 1 unrecorded unit(s) by client 0 before each sweep.
  - In-process network: clients, provers and the EVM share one Node process.
  - In-process EDR guard (EDR_GUARD=1, see installEdrGuard): nonce queries, gas estimates and calls use the "latest" block instead of "pending" (3122 queries retagged; each sender has at most one transaction in flight, so the nonce is the same), eth_estimateGas and eth_call run one at a time (0 + 9), and a full GC runs every second. EDR allocator: MIMALLOC_PURGE_DELAY=0.
